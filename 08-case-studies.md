# Case Studies and Drills

Use historical episodes to practice decisions without knowing the outcome. When possible, stop the data at the decision date before forming a view.

## Case 1 — Inflation Shock
Scenario: inflation repeatedly exceeds expectations while the central bank initially describes pressures as temporary.

Questions:
- What is priced?
- Front end or long end?
- Nominal or real rates?
- Curve shape?
- FX implications?
- Which trade has the cleanest payoff?

## Case 2 — Rapid Easing Cycle
Scenario: labour data deteriorate quickly and financial conditions tighten.

Construct:
- outright duration trade;
- curve trade;
- cross-market trade;
- FX trade.

Rank them.

## Case 3 — Canada/U.S. Divergence
Scenario: Canadian domestic demand weakens while U.S. growth remains resilient.

Evaluate:
- BoC/Fed differential;
- CAD;
- Canada-U.S. curves;
- equity/commodity confounds.

## Case 4 — Fiscal Expansion
Scenario: large unexpected fiscal expansion in an economy near capacity.

Map possible effects on:
- real growth;
- inflation;
- policy rate;
- term premium;
- curve;
- currency;
- equities.

Do not assume the sign of the currency response without explaining the dominant channel.

## Case 5 — Risk-Off Shock
Scenario: sudden global financial stress.

Identify trades that looked diversified ex ante but become correlated.

## Case 6 — Correct Forecast, Losing Trade
Invent a case where:
- GDP forecast is correct;
- central-bank forecast is correct;
- your bond position loses money.

Find at least four mechanisms.

## Case 7 — Losing Trade Post-Mortem
Choose one actual shadow-fund loser. Write the post-mortem before reviewing subsequent data.

## Case 8 — Investment Committee Challenge
Pitch a trade. Then answer:
- What is already priced?
- Why do you know better?
- Why now?
- Why this instrument?
- Why this size?
- What is the carry?
- What makes you wrong?
- What else in the portfolio has the same exposure?
- What is the downside if liquidity disappears?
- Why should I allocate scarce risk budget to this instead of doing nothing?
