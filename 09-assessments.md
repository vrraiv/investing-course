# Assessments and Rubrics

## Weekly Self-Test

Each week answer without notes:
1. Define the key concepts.
2. Calculate one representative problem.
3. Apply the concept to today's market.
4. Explain one common misuse.
5. Explain the concept to an investment committee in under 60 seconds.

Score each 0–2:
- 0 = cannot do;
- 1 = partial / needs reference;
- 2 = fluent.

Do not advance with <7/10.

## Midterm — Rates Trade

Submit a current-market trade.

Scoring:
| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Understanding of market pricing | 20% |
| Variant perception | 15% |
| Instrument/expression | 15% |
| Risk and sizing | 15% |
| Carry/roll | 10% |
| Catalysts/invalidation | 10% |
| Scenario analysis | 10% |
| Communication | 5% |

Pass: 75%.

## Portfolio Review

Every four weeks score:
- mandate compliance;
- risk discipline;
- thesis quality;
- diversification;
- attribution quality;
- willingness to exit;
- documentation integrity.

## Interview Question Bank

Be able to answer:
- What makes a good macro trade?
- How is being right on economics different from making money?
- Walk me through DV01.
- What is carry and roll?
- Why use a curve trade rather than an outright?
- How would you express BoC easing relative to the Fed?
- What can cause a steepener to lose money?
- How do you size a position?
- How do you know whether two trades are diversified?
- Explain VaR's weaknesses.
- Tell me about your worst investment decision.
- What evidence would make you abandon your highest-conviction view?
- How would a pension fund's objectives change the way you manage a macro portfolio?
- What is your investment edge?
- Why should we give you risk rather than hire another economist?

## Final Standard

You are "PM-interview ready" when you can:
- pitch any live position in 3 minutes;
- quantify its risk;
- state what is priced;
- explain the chosen expression;
- identify its correlation with the portfolio;
- give bull/base/bear P&L;
- state invalidation without hand-waving;
- discuss a losing trade without rewriting history.
