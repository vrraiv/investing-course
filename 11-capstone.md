# Capstone: Institutional Macro Investment Book

## Objective

Produce a package you could credibly discuss with a senior PM or CIO.

## Section 1 — Investment Philosophy
2 pages maximum.

Address:
- where you believe macro edge comes from;
- what markets best match your edge;
- discretionary vs systematic inputs;
- horizon;
- risk philosophy;
- conditions under which you should not trade.

## Section 2 — Shadow Fund
Include:
- mandate;
- cumulative return;
- monthly returns;
- volatility;
- Sharpe;
- maximum drawdown;
- gross/net risk;
- risk contribution;
- current exposures.

## Section 3 — Attribution
Show:
- asset-class attribution;
- theme attribution;
- winners/losers;
- carry vs repricing where possible;
- sizing contribution.

## Section 4 — Six Case Studies
Required mix:
- outright rates;
- curve;
- cross-market rates;
- FX;
- cross-asset or inflation;
- losing trade.

Each: 2 pages maximum.

## Section 5 — Quantitative Research
One complete project:
- question;
- economic rationale;
- data;
- methodology;
- out-of-sample design;
- results;
- limitations;
- portfolio use.

## Section 6 — Current Investment Committee
Prepare a 10-slide-equivalent narrative:
1. macro regime;
2. what markets price;
3. portfolio;
4. top risks;
5. highest-conviction opportunity;
6. trade;
7. sizing;
8. scenarios;
9. invalidation;
10. catalyst/exit.

## Final Oral

30 minutes:
- 5 min portfolio overview;
- 5 min trade pitch;
- 15 min challenge/Q&A;
- 5 min losing-trade post-mortem.

The goal is not to sound like an economist who learned finance vocabulary. The goal is to demonstrate a repeatable process for allocating risk.
