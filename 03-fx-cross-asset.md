# FX, Inflation and Cross-Asset Macro

## FX

### Core concepts
Master:
- spot and forward rates;
- base/quote currency;
- forward points;
- covered interest parity;
- carry;
- nominal and real effective exchange rates;
- purchasing-power parity as a long-horizon anchor;
- terms of trade;
- balance-of-payments channels;
- safe-haven dynamics;
- intervention.

### Investor framing

Never stop at "CAD should appreciate."

Ask:
1. Against what?
2. Over what horizon?
3. Is the rate differential helping or hurting?
4. Is valuation already extreme?
5. What catalyst closes the gap?
6. Is CAD the cleanest expression of the macro thesis?
7. What happens in a global risk-off shock?

## Inflation

Distinguish:
- nominal yields;
- real yields;
- breakeven inflation;
- expected inflation;
- inflation risk premium.

Practice decomposing statements such as:

"10-year yields rose because fiscal policy became more expansionary."

Possible channels:
- higher expected real growth;
- higher policy path;
- higher inflation expectations;
- higher inflation risk premium;
- higher real term premium;
- greater supply/fiscal risk.

A PM needs to know which channel is tradeable.

## Equities

- discount rates;
- equity risk premium;
- duration/growth stocks;
- cyclicals;
- financial conditions;
- index composition;

## Credit

Learn:
- government vs credit spread;
- investment grade vs high yield;
- default and liquidity premia;
- spread duration;
- credit as a growth/risk indicator;
- why falling government yields can coexist with losses in credit.
