# Resource Guide

Use editions currently available to you. The point is mastery, not completion of a reading list.

## Fixed Income
- Bruce Tuckman & Angel Serrat — *Fixed Income Securities*
- Frank Fabozzi — selected fixed-income reference material
- Moorad Choudhry — practical bond-market references

Priority topics: duration/DV01, forwards, curve construction, swaps, futures, carry/roll, relative value.

## Portfolio and Risk
- Grinold & Kahn — *Active Portfolio Management* (selected chapters)
- Antti Ilmanen — *Expected Returns*
- Antti Ilmanen — *Investing Amid Low Expected Returns* for broader institutional context
- Meucci — portfolio/risk material where useful

## Macro Investing
- Richard Clarida (ed.) / practitioner and central-bank material on monetary policy and markets
- Research from BIS, central banks, IMF and major asset managers
- Historical investment letters and post-mortems from macro managers where legally/publicly available

Avoid treating famous-investor anecdotes as a substitute for mechanics.

## Derivatives
Use a standard derivatives text such as Hull selectively:
- futures;
- forwards;
- swaps;
- option basics;
- Greeks.

You need investment fluency, not every pricing derivation.

## Data Sources
Potential free/accessible sources:
- Bank of Canada;
- Statistics Canada;
- Federal Reserve / FRED;
- U.S. Treasury;
- BLS/BEA;
- ECB;
- Eurostat;
- OECD;
- BIS;
- IMF;
- CFTC positioning data.

Where professional intraday or swap data are unavailable, document the limitation rather than fabricating precision.

## Reading Current Markets

For every research note/article, extract:
1. author's forecast;
2. market pricing cited;
3. variant perception;
4. catalyst;
5. recommended expression;
6. risk/invalidation.

This turns passive reading into PM training.

## Optional Credentials

CFA material can be used selectively to fill gaps in:
- fixed income;
- derivatives;
- portfolio management;
- ethics/institutional practice.

Do not let credential study displace the shadow portfolio and casebook.
