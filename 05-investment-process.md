# Investment Process

## The Core Funnel

### 1. Economic state
What is happening?

### 2. Forecast
What is likely to happen next?

### 3. Expectations
What does consensus believe?

### 4. Market pricing
What outcome/distribution is embedded in assets?

### 5. Variant perception
Where specifically do you disagree?

### 6. Catalyst
Why should pricing converge toward your view within your horizon?

### 7. Expression
Which instrument best isolates the disagreement?

### 8. Sizing
How much risk deserves to be allocated?

### 9. Monitoring
Which observations update the thesis?

### 10. Exit
Target, invalidation, horizon, or superior opportunity?

### 11. Attribution
Why did the trade make or lose money?

### 12. Learning
Was the process correct?

## Trade Memo Template

### Thesis
One sentence.

### Current pricing
State what the relevant market implies.

### My distribution
Provide at least bull/base/bear scenarios with probabilities.

### Variant perception
Precisely identify the disagreement.

### Catalyst
List events or information likely to resolve it.

### Expression
Instrument and direction.

### Alternatives considered
At least two.

### Why this expression?
Discuss purity, carry, convexity, liquidity and correlation.

### Position size
Notional plus risk measure.

### Expected payoff
Bull/base/bear P&L in bp of NAV.

### Carry/roll
Expected over intended horizon.

### Invalidation
Observable facts—not merely a price stop.

### Exit plan
Target/horizon/review date.

## Post-Mortem Template

Classify the result:
- thesis right / expression right;
- thesis right / expression wrong;
- thesis wrong / process good;
- thesis wrong / process bad;
- lucky win;
- unlucky loss.

Then ask:
1. Was market pricing measured correctly?
2. Did I identify the true catalyst?
3. Was sizing appropriate?
4. Was the position correlated with something I missed?
5. Did the data change or did I rationalize?
6. Would I take the trade again with the information available at entry?

## Weekly PM Routine

### Monday
Update portfolio exposures, pricing, catalysts and risk.

### During week
Log material data/events and thesis updates.

### Friday
Attribute weekly P&L and write 3–5 sentences per live thesis.

### Month-end
Produce:
- return;
- volatility;
- drawdown;
- attribution;
- exposure;
- winners/losers;
- process errors;
- next-month risk agenda.
